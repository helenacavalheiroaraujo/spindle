Story 29 · The page under the log-in window

A page that needs an account (checkout, cart checkout, cart, sell, Your account), opened by its address while signed out, shows its own empty state with the log-in window on top (decision 2026-11-14, choice 1b). Each option shows Your account (window without a record, B1) and a checkout (window with the record, B). The window is the one approved on 14 Nov; only the page under it changes. Illustrative data.
