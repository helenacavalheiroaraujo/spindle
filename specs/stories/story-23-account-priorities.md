# Story 23 · Buying priorities on the account page

Status: proposed. Design: docs/design/account-priorities.html (approved 2026-11-10).

## Goal
A signed-in buyer sees their saved buying priorities on the account page.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Signed in with a saved ranking: the account page shows "Buying priorities" with the saved order, numbered from 1.
- [ ] Signed in with no saved ranking: the section is not shown.
- [ ] Signed out: the account page redirects to log in (as today).
- [ ] The section only shows; there is no way to change the ranking from the account page.

## Out of scope
- Changing the ranking from the account page (the ranking is changed on the album's priorities screen, story 2).
