# Bug 7 · Protection steps look like tasks for the user

> Found in feedback on the app · 2026-11-10. Status: open.

## Description
On the checkout page, the "What protection covers" block shows four numbered steps. Users who do not read the text think they have to do four things and get confused about whether they need to do anything there.

## Steps to reproduce
Go through the flow up to the checkout.

## Current vs expected behaviour
**Current:** The four numbered steps, with step 1 highlighted, look like a to-do list for the user. Users who do not read the text think they have to do more things and get confused.
**Expected:** The block shows only a single line, "How protection works". The four steps are hidden and open when the user clicks it.

## Customer impact
Poor UX.

## Priority
P4
