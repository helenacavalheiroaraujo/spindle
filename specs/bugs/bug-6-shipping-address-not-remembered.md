# Bug 6 · Shipping address is not remembered between purchases

> Found in feedback on the app · 2026-11-10. Status: open.

## Description
After I have already made a purchase, I have to fill in the shipping address again when I want to buy again.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Complete a purchase.
2. Buy another record.
3. At the checkout, the "Ship to" fields (Name, Street, Postcode, City) are empty.

## Current vs expected behaviour
**Current:** The shipping address has to be filled in again on every purchase.
**Expected:** The address from the previous purchase is already filled in.

## Customer impact
Poor UX.

## Priority
P3
